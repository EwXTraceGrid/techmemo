📈 WHY NOW
→ The number of drones on the battlefield is growing exponentially. Standard ground-based direction finders cannot keep pace with threat mobility.
→ DJI and FPV drones operate at 2.4 / 5.8 GHz — a range many ground-based direction finders do not cover.
→ An airborne direction finder can observe hundreds of sq. km, while a ground unit is horizon-limited. This is a fundamentally different coverage area.
→ Regulators and authorities (Bundesnetzagentur, EASA, Ministries of Defence) are creating demand for certified airborne monitoring solutions.
→ The operational EwX v0.1 prototype already demonstrates the concept. Investment in v0.2 is the transition from PoC to an operational product.
